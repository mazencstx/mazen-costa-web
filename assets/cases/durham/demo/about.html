DURHAM

Egypt Streetwear. Nothing Extra.

DURHAM is an Egyptian streetwear label built on restraint. No prints, no color, no noise — just black, white, and clean silhouettes cut for the way Egypt actually moves.

Every piece is oversized or regular-fit, heavyweight cotton, made close to home. The name on the tag stays quiet. The fit does the talking.

We believe the fewer decisions a garment forces on you, the longer it stays in rotation. That's the whole philosophy — nothing extra.

Black & White Only

No exceptions. Two colors, done properly, is more than enough.

Made in Egypt

Produced locally, cut for local bodies and local heat.

Heavyweight Cotton

Built to hold shape wash after wash — no thin, disposable fabric.

No Loud Branding

The DURHAM name lives on the inside label, not across your chest.
